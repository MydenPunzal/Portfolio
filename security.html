Security

Nothing to leak, by design

FlashlyV1 is built so a breach of its own systems can't expose your leads' personal information.

No PII, ever

No names, emails, or phone numbers stored

FlashlyV1 only ever holds two things: your client configuration, and anonymous de-dup fingerprints. Every lead is validated and forwarded through a signed webhook, so your CRM receives it and FlashlyV1 doesn't keep a copy.
