How It Works

From raw lead to routed lead

NO dashboard to learn,setup,and worry about. FlashlyV1 runs quietly in the background.

Unli leads, One webhook

Validate → dedupe → sign → route

A lead comes in from any connected source. Flashlight checks it's a real, valid entry, compares it against an anonymous de-dup fingerprint so duplicates get caught, then forwards it through a signed webhook straight to your CRM. Matched to the client it belongs to.
