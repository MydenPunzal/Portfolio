What FlashlyV1 actually does

One job: Catch bad leads before your team or your client ever sees them.

Validate, De-dupe, Route

FlashlyV1 sits in front of your funnel

Every incoming lead is checked for validity and compared against an anonymous de-dup fingerprint, so the same person can't slip through twice. Once cleared, it's forwarded through a signed webhook straight to your CRM.
